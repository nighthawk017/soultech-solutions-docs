Delete Your Data

Monsters vs. Ski Poles · Soul Tech Solutions

In short: the game has no accounts. If you agreed to share anonymous play data, you can stop it and delete what was collected from inside the game, in Settings. Your saved progress stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

Delete anonymous play data

The game only sends anonymous gameplay statistics if you chose "Share" when it asked. To delete them:

  1. Open Monsters vs. Ski Poles.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Anonymous play data so it shows off.

The game stops sending statistics straight away and asks Unity Gaming Services, which processes the statistics for us, to delete all data collected for your device's random player ID. Do this before uninstalling the game: the player ID is stored only in the app, so once the app is removed, neither you nor we can tell which data was yours.

If you never chose "Share", nothing was sent and there is nothing to delete.

Delete game progress saved on your device

Stars, scores, streaks, bestiary entries and settings are saved only on your device; we never receive them. Delete them by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings › Apps › Monsters vs. Ski Poles › Storage › Clear storage) or by uninstalling the game. In the web version, clear the site's data in your browser.

What is deleted and what is kept

A deletion request removes all anonymous play data linked to your player ID. We keep nothing else about you, because we never collect your name, email or other contact details. For more details, see the privacy policy.

Privacy Policy

Monsters vs. Ski Poles · Soul Tech Solutions · Effective 13 November 2026

In short: Monsters vs. Ski Poles has no accounts, ads or in-app purchases. If you say yes when the game asks, it sends anonymous gameplay statistics (for example how far up the lift you got) so we can make the game fairer. If you say no, nothing is sent. You can change your answer at any time in Settings. We never sell data.

Who we are

Monsters vs. Ski Poles (the "game") is made by Soul Tech Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles information on Android and in the web version.

Anonymous gameplay statistics (optional)

The first time you open the game, it asks whether you want to share anonymous play data. Only if you choose "Share", the game sends us statistics about how you play, using Unity Gaming Services Analytics, a service run by Unity Technologies for us:

  • rides: the mode and lift, whether you won, lost or left, your score, how far up the lift you got, riders saved and caught, poles thrown and monsters hit;
  • which kind of monster caught a rider and when;
  • tutorial steps finished, retried or skipped;
  • basic technical information Unity adds to every event: a random player ID made by the game, a session ID, the game version, the device model, operating system, language and approximate country (worked out from the internet connection).

We use this only to find where the game is too hard or too easy and to fix it. It does not include your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID, and we do not try to work out who you are. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell or share it with anyone else.

Your choice: turn "Anonymous play data" off in Settings at any time. The game then stops sending statistics and asks Unity to delete the data already collected for your player ID. If you never say yes, nothing is ever sent.

Unity processes this data on our behalf under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Data is sent over an encrypted connection.

Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game also saves a small amount of game data locally on your device only:

  • campaign stars and best scores, daily ride scores, tries and streak;
  • bestiary entries you have discovered and whether you finished the tutorial;
  • your settings, such as sound volume, mute, throwing controls and your play data choice.

This data stays on your device and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game (in a browser, by clearing the site's data).

Third parties

Apart from Unity, which processes the optional statistics for us as described above, we do not share data with third parties. When you download the game from Google Play, Google may process information under its own Privacy Policy; that processing is not controlled by us. The same applies to the website you play the web version on.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. The optional statistics are anonymous and are only sent after someone chooses "Share". If you are a parent and want nothing sent, choose "No thanks" or turn "Anonymous play data" off in Settings.

Your rights

We do not hold your name or contact details, so we cannot link statistics to you. You can stop collection and have the collected statistics deleted at any time with the Settings switch. Your locally saved game data is under your control as described above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled (for example by adding online leaderboards), we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
